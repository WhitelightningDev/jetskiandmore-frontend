SAFETY STANDARD

Watch. Sign. Get briefed. Then ride.

SAMSA registered and recertified every year, with certified skippers on every launch. Here's exactly what happens between arriving and riding.

STEP 1 · BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

The safety video (6 minutes)

Sent with your booking confirmation. Covers throttle and steering behaviour, the riding zone, no-wake areas, hand signals, and what to do if you come off. Watch it at home so your slot is spent on the water, not in a briefing room.

STEP 2 · DIGITAL INDEMNITY

Sign once, on your phone

Every rider signs the indemnity after the video — under-18s need a guardian's signature. No paperwork at the slipway.
